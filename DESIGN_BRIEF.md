# Margin — Design Brief

**Margin** is an offline, privacy-first Android app that blocks distracting apps *and* helps you actually improve over time. No accounts, nothing leaves the device. Design a clean, calm mobile app for it.

**Core idea:** stop you in the moment of temptation, anchor it to who you want to become, and review your progress weekly.

## Features

- **App blocker (Shield):** create rules that block chosen apps on a schedule (time slots + days of week). Two modes — **Block** (a hard stop) or **Friction** (a ~12-second breathing pause, then an honest choice). A "commitment lock" can make a rule un-disable-able while it's active.

- **Intercept screen:** when you open a blocked app, a full-screen moment appears — it reminds you of your personal "why," and offers a better habit to do instead.

- **Focus now:** instantly block your distractions for 25 / 45 / 60 minutes, with a live countdown.

- **Identity:** you set "I'm someone who…" — every boundary points back to it.

- **Habits:** small replacement behaviours with daily check-ins, streaks, and a contribution heatmap.

- **Daily reflection:** a 10-second evening check-in — how close was today to who you're becoming.

- **Weekly Review:** a plain-language recap of your week, a reflection prompt, and one focus to set for next week.

- **Insights:** patterns from your data ("Instagram pulls at you most late at night") with a one-tap fix, plus a 14-day trend.

- **Onboarding:** set your identity, then grant the permissions that make blocking work.

## Tone

Calm, minimal, editorial. Light theme. No gamified hype.

## Screens to design

Today (home), Shield, Habits, Review, Insights — plus Onboarding, the Intercept screen, and the rule-creation flow.
