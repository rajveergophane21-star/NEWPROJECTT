# Privacy Policy — Margin

**Last updated: 28 June 2026**

Margin ("the app") is a privacy-first, fully offline app blocker and habit tracker. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

## The short version

**Margin collects no personal data, sends nothing off your device, and has no servers, accounts, analytics, ads, or trackers.** Everything you create — your blocks and your habits — is stored only on your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** Margin does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device data. The app has no internet permission and makes no network connections.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally (in the app's private storage), and only on your device:

- The apps, schedules, and modes for the blocks you create.
- The habits you add and the days you check them off.

This data never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it.

## Permissions and why they are needed

Margin requests a few Android permissions strictly to make on-device blocking work. None of these are used to collect or transmit data:

- **Accessibility service** — used **only** to detect *which app is currently in the foreground*, so the app can show your block screen at the right moment. Margin does **not** read screen content, text, passwords, or any information inside other apps. This is a non‑data, on‑device signal.
- **Display over other apps (overlay)** — to show the block/friction screen on top of a blocked app.
- **Usage access** — an optional fallback method of detecting the foreground app when the accessibility service is unavailable.
- **Notifications** — to show the permanent, quiet notification Android requires for the background service that keeps blocking active.
- **Run at startup** — to restore your blocks after the phone reboots.

## Data sharing

We do not share any data with anyone, because we do not collect any.

## Children

Margin does not collect data from anyone, including children, and contains no ads.
